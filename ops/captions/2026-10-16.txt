Your squat isn't stuck. Your bracing is.

Most lifters who stall at the same weight for months don't need more volume. They're losing pressure at the bottom, and the bar tells them about it.

1. Breathe into your belly, not your chest. If your shoulders rise, you lost it.
2. Brace before you unrack, not halfway down.
3. Hold the breath through the bottom. Most people let it go at the hardest point.
4. Reset your breath between reps. Under 5 reps, one breath per rep.
